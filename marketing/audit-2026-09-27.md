# Template audit — 27 Sep 2026

**What was asked.** A complete audit of the template: what is missing, what
is over-engineered, what should be removed. **Method.** Three read-only
scans (scripts, workflows and hooks · `src/`, `public/` and the worker ·
every doc and skill), each finding checked against the file before it was
acted on. Nothing was adopted or removed on a scan's word alone.

**What happened to the findings.** Clear-cut defects are fixed in this
branch (§ 1). Removals with no judgement in them are done (§ 2). Everything
that changes structure, drops a feature or costs more than an hour is a
recommendation with an effort estimate (§ 3–5), and § 6 is the list of
decisions the owner takes. Once a decision is taken it moves to
`CHECKLIST.md` with its reason and this file's line is struck.

## 6. Decisions for the owner

Each with the recommended answer. Reply with the number and yes/no/other.

1. **Do O2 + O3** (shared script libs and one collections config)? Yes.
   It is the change that stops the next enum drift; a day of work, three
   parity rules and eleven `walk()`s retired.
2. **Do O1** (one scorer per question)? Yes. `npm run audit:discovery`
   becomes the off-site half of `npm run aeo`.
3. **Do M2** (a money-page scaffold and a comparison layout)? Yes. Half
   the engine assumes it; without it `claimFrom` stays empty on every
   site.
4. **Do M3** (About page to the entity spec, Key Facts in `facts.json`)?
   Yes, with `/new-site` phase 10 filling it.
5. **Add tests (M1)**? Yes, `node --test`, wired into pre-commit.
6. **Doc consolidation (O12, § 5)**? Yes, three PRs. Expected: the doc
   set drops from about 68,000 to about 45,000 words with nothing lost.
7. **Cut the IndexNow race machinery (O4) and the zip reader (O5)?** Yes
   to both; keep `--changed` only if Actions come back.
8. **Restore the CI push trigger for this repository?** No, unless
   Actions minutes exist; the pre-push battery is the gate. If yes, M6
   also resolves itself.
9. **Twin routing cost.** `/blog/*` and `/glossary/*` pass every HTML view
   through the metered worker to serve `Accept: text/markdown`. Keep
   (100k free invocations a day is far above a small site's traffic) or
   move the twins to `/blog/<slug>.md` static files and drop the routes.
   Recommendation: keep until the free tier is in sight; note it.
10. **Date drift in `lastmod.json`**: keep it a note (current) or make it
    fail? Recommendation: keep it a note; missing routes already fail.
