# Field notes

The proprietary feed. Dated entries from the real world — meetings, calls,
site visits, support threads, things noticed that no LLM could produce —
captured by the /interview skill (or by hand) and consumed by /write-content.
An entry here is what a post's `proprietary` frontmatter points at.

Format, newest first:

```
## YYYY-MM-DD — short title
status: unused | drafted:<slug> | published:<slug> | background
What happened, in enough detail that a piece can be written from it without
asking again. Names and figures that may NOT be published are marked
[private] inline — the writing skill must respect the mark.
Angle: the piece this could fuel, one line. (Optional.)
```

Rules:

- `background` means context that informs writing but must never become a
  piece on its own.
- The writing skill flips `status` when it uses an entry, so nothing is used
  twice and unused fuel is visible at a glance.
- Nothing here renders anywhere. It is an internal record — but it is in the
  repo, so keep out anything that cannot be in the repo.
