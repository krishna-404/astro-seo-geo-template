## 9. What we refuse

- 3D / WebGL scenes, scroll-jacking, kinetic body copy, glassmorphism outside
  the header — they fail Core Web Vitals or readers.
- Stock photography; generated people; `<div>` screenshots.
- A brand colour the contrast sweep rejects; pure black; the two default
  palettes (purple-glow, cream-and-brass) unless the brand's own.
- Gradient text, neon glow, `transition: all`, emoji as icons, a label
  over every heading, section numbers, scroll cues, more than one marquee,
  three identical cards in a row, the same band twice in a row.
- TODO — the brand's own no-gos (interview question 7).
